About us

Freight transportation built around safety, reliability, and the right equipment.

Heavy Trail Logistics provides dependable freight transportation solutions across the United States, connecting businesses and loads with the right equipment for safe and efficient delivery. Our approach is straightforward: match each shipment with the appropriate transportation solution and support customers with responsive communication throughout the process.

We work with businesses that need practical freight support across a wide range of load types, route demands, and delivery timelines. Whether the need is a specialized equipment solution or a dependable move for time-sensitive freight, Heavy Trail Logistics is ready to help coordinate the transportation plan.

Our services

Transportation solutions for a wide range of freight needs.

01

Flatbed

Open-deck transportation suited for construction materials, machinery, and other freight that requires easy loading access.

02

Reefer / Refrigerated

Temperature-sensitive freight solutions for products that require controlled transport conditions.

03

Step Deck / Drop Deck

Lower-deck equipment options for taller loads, oversized freight, and cargo that needs additional clearance.

04

Hot Shot

Fast-moving, time-sensitive freight support for urgent loads that require quick turnaround and flexible scheduling.

05

Conestoga

Protected hauling solutions that combine the ease of open-deck access with added weather and cargo coverage.

06

Removable Gooseneck (RGN)

Heavy-haul transportation for equipment and freight that requires specialized loading and unloading capability.

07

Tanker

Liquid and bulk freight transportation support for cargo that requires equipment designed for specialized handling.

08

Box Truck

Efficient hauling for smaller freight loads where enclosed delivery and dependable handling are key priorities.
